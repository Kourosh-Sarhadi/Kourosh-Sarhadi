## ✔️ About Me

💧 My core interest lies in **hydrology** and the broader field of water resources management, understanding how water moves through rivers, aquifers, and reservoirs, and how we can manage these systems more effectively under increasing uncertainty and climate variability.

🤖 More specifically, I'm interested in the application of **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to water resources engineering**. This includes areas such as streamflow forecasting, reservoir operation optimization, groundwater modeling, and water quality prediction. I see AI not as a replacement for hydrological theory, but as a complementary tool that helps extract patterns from large hydrological datasets that classical methods often struggle to capture.

📚 I'm continuously working to build a stronger foundation in machine learning, with the long-term goal of applying these methods rigorously and meaningfully to real water resources problems.

---

## 👨🏻‍💻 Technical Expertise

🐍 I use **Python** for data analysis, scripting, and building predictive models, particularly for processing and analyzing hydrological datasets.

🗺️ I also work with **GIS** for spatial analysis of watersheds, catchments, and groundwater systems, since spatial context is essential in most water resources problems.

🛠️ In addition, I'm proficient in several **specialized software tools used in water resources engineering and management**, applied for hydrologic and hydraulic modeling, simulation, and decision-support purposes.

🤖 I'm currently expanding my skills in **Machine Learning**, with the goal of integrating these techniques into water resources modeling and analysis.
